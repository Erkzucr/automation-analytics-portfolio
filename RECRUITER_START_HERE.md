# If you're reviewing this for a role

Senior accounting and finance background, now working across automation, analytics and controls. This repo is the evidence.

Five minutes:

1. The [main README](README.md) for the overview and the charts.
2. [Case 05](case-studies/05-source-to-record-reconciliation/README.md), the clearest end-to-end reconciliation.
3. For AI, Copilot or transformation roles, [case 11](case-studies/11-copilot-studio-finance-agent/README.md) and its `agent-config/guardrails.md`.
4. For FP&A or analysis, [case 12](case-studies/12-people-cost-variance-analysis/README.md). For finance project management, [case 13](case-studies/13-close-improvement-project-tracker/README.md). Both end with what the output says about the data.
5. For proof instead of diagrams, `python run_case.py all --check` in `demo/case-pipeline` regenerates every case's output and compares it with what's committed.
6. Cases 07, 08 and 09 cover governance, control evidence, and AI with a person signing off.

Roles this maps to: finance automation, finance transformation, senior accounting, financial controls, FP&A and finance data analytics, finance project management, financial systems, process improvement, Copilot and Power Platform enablement for finance teams.
