# Recruiter Brief: Close Improvement Project Tracker

**Short version:** Earned value, milestone health and a scored risk register for a finance transformation project, with the status report generated from the plan.

**Situation:** A synthetic project to redesign accruals, automate reconciliations and build a close dashboard, tracked in a plan and a risk register.

**Task:** Give the sponsor a status that is calculated, comparable week to week, and ends with the decisions they need to make.

**Action:** PV, EV, AC, SPI, CPI, EAC and VAC per task, workstream and total; milestone states from dates; risk scoring and sorting; a markdown report generated from all of it, with tie-outs and tests.

**Result:** On the sample data, Build is behind (SPI 0.81), cost is slightly over (CPI 0.92), and the two decisions listed are UAT timing and a one-person dependency.
