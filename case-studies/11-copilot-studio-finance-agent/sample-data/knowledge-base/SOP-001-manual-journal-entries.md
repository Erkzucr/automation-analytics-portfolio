# SOP-001 Manual Journal Entries (synthetic)

Owner: Finance process owner (synthetic). Version 1.2. All thresholds and names are made up for this portfolio.

## 1. Purpose
Defines how manual journal entries are prepared, supported, approved, and posted.

## 2. Preparation
2.1 Every manual JE needs a description that a reviewer can understand without asking the preparer.
2.2 Supporting documentation is attached before submission. Screenshots of a spreadsheet are not support; the spreadsheet is.
2.3 Reversing entries are flagged at creation with the reversal period.

## 3. Approval tiers
3.1 Entries up to 10,000 in reporting currency: approved by the team lead.
3.2 Entries above 10,000 and up to 100,000: approved by the accounting manager.
3.3 Entries above 100,000: approved by the accounting manager and the controller.
3.4 The preparer can never approve their own entry.

## 4. Posting
4.1 Entries are posted only after approval is recorded in the system.
4.2 Late entries after close day 4 require controller approval regardless of amount.

## 5. Review
5.1 A sample of posted manual entries is reviewed monthly for support quality and correct approval tier.
