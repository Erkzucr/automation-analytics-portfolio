# SOP-002 Balance Sheet Reconciliations (synthetic)

Owner: Finance process owner (synthetic). Version 2.0. Everything here is made up for this portfolio.

## 1. Purpose
Defines the standard for reconciling balance sheet accounts to supporting detail.

## 2. Template
2.1 All reconciliations use the standard template stored in the shared reconciliation library.
2.2 The template has three sections: GL balance, supporting detail, and reconciling items with explanation and owner.

## 3. Timing
3.1 High-risk accounts are reconciled monthly by close day 6.
3.2 Low-risk accounts are reconciled quarterly by day 8 of the quarter-end close.

## 4. Open items
4.1 Any reconciling item older than 60 days needs a written action plan and a named owner.
4.2 Items older than 90 days are escalated to the controller and considered for write-off or adjustment.
4.3 "Plug" entries to force a reconciliation to balance are not permitted.

## 5. Review
5.1 Every reconciliation is reviewed by someone other than the preparer.
5.2 Reviewer sign-off is recorded in the reconciliation tool with a date.
