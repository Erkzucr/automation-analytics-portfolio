# SOP-003 Month-End Close Calendar (synthetic)

Owner: Finance process owner (synthetic). Version 1.0. Day numbers are working days after period end and are made up for this portfolio.

| Close day | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Sub-ledger cutoff (AP, AR, payroll, fixed assets) | Sub-ledger owners |
| Day 2 | Accruals and prepaid amortization posted | Accounting team |
| Day 3 | Intercompany matching complete | Intercompany team |
| Day 4 | Manual JE cutoff. Late entries need controller approval | Accounting team |
| Day 5 | Flux analysis and first review of results | Accounting manager |
| Day 6 | High-risk reconciliations complete | Preparers and reviewers |
| Day 7 | Reporting package issued | Controller |

Accrual deadline: all accruals must be submitted by end of day 2 for posting.
