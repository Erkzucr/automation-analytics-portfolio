# Recruiter Brief: Balanced Output Preparation

**Short version:** Taking messy detail data and turning it into a balanced, reviewable summary without losing the drill-back to source.

**Situation:** Detail records had inconsistent sign conventions and precision, making straight aggregation unreliable.

**Task:** Produce a summary output that balances and can be traced back to detail on demand.

**Action:** Normalized values, assigned reporting dimensions, aggregated, and added a tie-out check between summary and detail as a hard gate before anything gets published.

**Result:** A repeatable pattern for going from noisy detail to a balanced, audit-ready summary, shown here on synthetic data.
