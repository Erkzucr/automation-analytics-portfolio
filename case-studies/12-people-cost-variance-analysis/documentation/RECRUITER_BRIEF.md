# Recruiter Brief: People Cost Variance Analysis

**Short version:** Budget against actual for people cost, split into headcount, rate, mix and one-offs, with materiality by amount and by percent, and KPIs computed from the same lines as the detail.

**Situation:** Monthly variance explanations for the largest opex line were built by hand and changed with the preparer.

**Task:** Make the explanation mechanical, keep the review list short, and keep the KPIs consistent with the detail.

**Action:** Decomposition that ties to the total on every line, materiality on both an amount and a percentage, and the bridge chart and KPI page generated from one script with tests.

**Result:** On the synthetic data, 108 lines reduce to nine material ones, seven of them headcount-driven. The bridge from budget to actual is one chart.
