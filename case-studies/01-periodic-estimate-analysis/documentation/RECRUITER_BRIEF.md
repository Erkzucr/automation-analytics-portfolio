# Recruiter Brief: Periodic Estimate Analysis

**The short version:** I took a recurring estimate process that would normally get rebuilt by hand each period and turned it into a validated, controlled workflow with visible exceptions and a final tie-out.

**Situation:** Recurring estimates that pull from multiple sources tend to accumulate manual checks over time, and those checks live in someone's head rather than in the process.

**Task:** Build something repeatable that a reviewer could pick up and trust without re-deriving the logic from scratch.

**Action:** I split the process into distinct stages, validate inputs, standardize fields, apply the calculation, and route anything questionable to a single exception register instead of leaving it buried in the output.

**Result:** A synthetic demonstration of the pattern, with a summary that ties back to the accepted detail every time it runs.
