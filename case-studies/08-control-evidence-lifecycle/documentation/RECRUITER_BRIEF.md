# Recruiter Brief: Control Evidence and Assurance Lifecycle

**Short version:** Making sure a control's execution, exceptions, and remediation are all captured in one traceable place instead of scattered across trackers and email.

**Situation:** Control evidence was fragmented, ownership in one place, exceptions in another, retention rules nowhere consistent.

**Task:** Build a lifecycle that carries a control from execution through remediation to sign-off without losing the trail.

**Action:** Defined evidence requirements per control, reviewed execution against them, tracked remediation on failures, and rolled everything into a certification summary.

**Result:** A control evidence pattern where every control's status, and the evidence behind it, can be reconstructed on demand.
